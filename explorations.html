A note on the process

Good design isn't only about knowing what works. It's also about being willing to try what might.

This space is for learning by doing—trying unfamiliar approaches, combining unexpected ideas and developing my visual language one experiment at a time. Not everything needs to become a finished project. Sometimes the exploration itself is the point.
